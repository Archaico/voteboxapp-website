Installing on Android — a quick walkthrough

Because the app isn’t in the Play Store yet, your phone will show a couple of caution screens. Here’s exactly what to tap.

✓
This is normal — nothing is wrong with the app. Android shows these warnings for any app installed outside the Play Store, including ones still in testing. VoteBoxApp is fully open source — every line is public on GitHub.
1

“Unsafe app blocked”

When this appears, don’t press OK. Instead, tap the small Install anyway text just above the button.

Tap “Install anyway”
2

“Send app for a security check?”

You don’t need to send anything to Google. Just tap Don’t send and the install will finish on its own.

Tap “Don’t send”
3

Open & you’re in

The app installs, opens to the VoteBoxApp welcome screen, and asks for your fingerprint. That’s it — you’re ready to vote.

Done 🌱
