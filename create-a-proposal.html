🤔 The Challenge: Who Pays?
Voters never pay - that's VoteBoxApp's core promise. But proposal creation requires:
• Gas fees (~$0.10-0.50 in ADA) for blockchain transaction
• Foundation Fee (25% markup) to sustain development
• Total cost: ~$0.13-0.63 per proposal
The Solution: Proposal creators pay, but we make it accessible through TWO payment options:
Option 1: Pay with Credit/Debit Card Stay on Tier 1 (email + biometric). No wallet needed. Maximum accessibility.
Option 2: Pay with Cardano Wallet Upgrade to Tier 2 for self-sovereign identity. Pay directly in ADA. Web3 native.

✨ Key Takeaways
Two payment paths maintain accessibility:
💳 Card Payment (Most Users) • Fastest and simplest (2 minutes)
• No blockchain knowledge needed
• Stay on Tier 1 (email + biometric)
• Cost: $0.52 (includes processing fee)
👛 Wallet Payment (Web3 Native) • Upgrade to self-sovereign identity
• Get W3C DID (portable across platforms)
• Lower fees (no card processing)
• Cost: $0.50 in ADA
Strategic Benefits:
✅ Voters never pay (free voting for all)
✅ Creators choose their preferred payment method
✅ No wallet required to participate (maximum accessibility)
✅ Optional upgrade path to Tier 2 for those who want it
✅ Foundation Fee sustains development without VC funding
