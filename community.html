Tap into a proposal's Discussion tab. You see comments from app users (by pseudonym) and Discord users (with a badge). You can reply to either.

🤖
What is the VoteBoxApp Bot?
A small software program running on a server — not your phone — connected to both systems simultaneously. Think of it as a bilingual translator: it speaks VoteBoxApp and Discord fluently, relaying messages between the two worlds automatically, 24/7, no human needed.
🌉
Why Discord specifically?
Discord is where an enormous number of crypto, DAO, activist, and community groups already communicate daily — roughly 200 million registered users. By bridging to Discord, VoteBoxApp proposals reach those communities without requiring everyone to download a new app first.
⚖️
Who can actually vote?
Only verified VoteBoxApp app users with connected wallets. Discord participation is discussion-only. This preserves the integrity of the democratic process while still allowing broad community engagement and awareness.
🏛️
The Town Hall Metaphor
The VoteBoxApp is the town hall — where verified citizens debate and cast votes. Discord is the public square outside — where the broader community hears what is being debated. They can be heard inside, but they cannot pull a voting lever. The Bot shuttles messages between the two rooms.
🔮
Phase 1 vs Phase 2
Your current working APK is Phase 1 — in-app discussions only, no Discord yet. Discord integration is Phase 2: a planned enhancement the architecture is already designed for. Phase 1 is a working democracy. Phase 2 adds the public square.
