VoteBoxApp uses Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) to verify your eligibility without revealing your identity. Your vote is protected by the same mathematics that secures cryptocurrencies and military communications.

ZK Proof Steps:

⏳ Retrieve your secret from secure storage
⏳ Load Merkle tree from IPFS
⏳ Generate ZK proof of membership
⏳ Create unique nullifier (prevents double voting)
⏳ Submit encrypted vote to blockchain
Why does this take time?

Zero-Knowledge Proofs require complex mathematical computations. Your phone is performing advanced cryptography that proves your eligibility without revealing any private information. This typically takes 10-20 seconds on mobile devices.
